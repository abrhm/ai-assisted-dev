argument-hint: <description of upcoming changes>
effort: high
---

Do not create a plan yet. First, build full understanding through discussion.

Ask questions with numbered lettered options. A = recommended. Example:

**1. Question?**
A. Recommended option
B. Alternative

User replies compactly: `1A, 2B` or `2: discuss` for further discussion.

If a question can be answered by exploring the codebase, explore it instead of asking.

When shared understanding is reached, enter plan mode.
